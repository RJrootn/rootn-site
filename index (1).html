Responsible AI

Responsible AI isn't a policy page. It's how we build.

Four commitments run through every rootn.ai product, and each one shows up in how the product actually works.

Grounded and transparent

AI that shows its evidence and its limits.

  • Automatic evidence checks on every Samyoga review
  • No made-up scores or match percentages

Private by design

People decide what we keep, and can delete it.

  • Personal details removed before AI review where possible
  • No ads, no selling data

Humans stay in control

AI drafts and suggests. People decide.

  • Your CV is never rewritten; you edit every line
  • You approve content before it is published

Fair to the people we serve

Value first, and honest pricing.

  • Free previews before you pay
  • Pay per use where a subscription would not be fair
AI can be wrong. When ours is, we want to know. We are honest about what is still in beta, and we fix what people report.
